Commitment

Did the sprint achieve what it was for? That is a different question from whether the points landed, and only the team can answer it — a sprint can finish 95% of its commitment and miss the one outcome it was planned around, or drop half its points and still deliver the goal. Left as Not recorded it counts in nothing.

Change During the Sprint

Rolling 5

For a sprint that would mislead the forecast — a major incident that took it over, a re-baselining of the points, or a sprint most of the team was away for. The sprint's own figures are untouched and every other view still shows them; only the rolling 5 and the capacity target skip it, and both say that they have.

100% is a normal sprint. As a rough guide, one person in five away for the whole sprint is about 80%. Over 100% is allowed for the rarer case of extra help or a longer sprint.

The Sprints Behind the Figure

The recommendation is the average of these sprints. If one of them was produced by a different team — three people then, four now — scale it up or down and the average moves with it. Only this recommendation changes: the sprint's own recorded figures stay exactly as they are everywhere else in the app.

How Long For

This governs the availability figure at the top. Leave it unticked for a one-off — leave, public holidays, a shorter sprint — and it applies to this sprint only. Tick it for a lasting change that should hold until you change it. Sprint scaling needs no such choice: a scale stays on its sprint until you change it, and retires naturally when that sprint drops out of the window.

PI Objectives

Business Value

Add up the business value your ART assigned to this team's objectives at PI planning. Planned covers the objectives the team committed to — leave stretch objectives out of it, they were never a commitment. Achieved is how much of that committed value landed. From stretch is what the stretch objectives delivered on top: it counts in the measure and never in the commitment, which is what lets an honest figure sit above 100%.
