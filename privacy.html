Sprint Velocity — Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Sprint Velocity is built to work entirely in your browser. This page explains exactly what data exists, where it goes, and who can see it.

What Sprint Velocity stores

The only data the app holds is what you type in: team names, PI names, sprint numbers and dates, the seven sprint figures (committed, completed, added, removed, carried in and out, velocity), and any sprint notes — plus your preferences (theme, which view was open). There is no account, profile, or usage tracking required to use the app.

Where that data lives

In your browser (always). Everything is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This is the only copy that exists if you never use the two optional features below. Clearing the site's browser data deletes it.

Google sign-in / Firestore (optional, off until you choose it). If you tap "Sign in to sync", your sprint data is stored in a Firebase/Firestore database operated by the developer so the same data can appear on your other devices, and Firebase Authentication records your Google account's email address and display name. Access rules restrict every account to its own data — other signed-in users cannot read yours. This only happens if you sign in; the app never does it on its own.

Share links (nothing is uploaded). A read-only share link carries the data inside the link itself, compressed into the part of the address that is never sent to any server. Creating or opening one involves no upload, no account, and no storage on the reader's device. Anyone who has the link can read what you chose to include, so treat the link like the data it contains — you pick which teams go in, and sprint notes are left out unless you opt in.

What Sprint Velocity does not do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking scripts
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing data with third parties
  • The "Recent changes" list fetches this app's own public commit history from GitHub's API; nothing about you is sent with that request

Your choices

You can edit or delete anything in the app at any time. Signing out stops all syncing and leaves the local copy in charge. To have your synced copy deleted from the developer's Firestore database, email eagleadams86@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
