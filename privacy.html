Sprint Velocity — Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Sprint Velocity is built to work entirely in your browser. This page explains exactly what data exists, where it goes, and who can see it.

What Sprint Velocity Stores

The only data the app holds is what you type in: team, ART and PI names, sprint numbers and dates, and the sprint figures (committed, completed, added, removed, carried in and out, velocity) — plus your preferences (theme, which view was open). Capacity adjustments add a percentage (how available a team will be for a sprint, or how a past sprint should be re-weighted for planning) and, where you record why, a code picked from a fixed list of options such as "annual leave or sickness". There is deliberately no free-text field anywhere: beyond those short names, only numbers, dates and those fixed codes are ever saved. (Earlier versions had optional sprint notes; the field was removed, and any notes in a previously saved copy are deleted the next time the app opens.) Text pasted from a Jira Sprint Report is read for its figures on the spot and never stored. There is no account, profile, or usage tracking required to use the app.

Where That Data Lives

In your browser. That is the only place. Everything is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. There is no server, no database and no account — the app has nowhere to send your data even if it wanted to. Clearing the site's browser data deletes it.

A copy of the app itself, so it opens offline. The page, its stylesheet, its chart library and its icon are kept on your device — the same public files anyone can read on GitHub, and nothing else. Your sprint figures are never put there. Clearing the site's browser data removes it along with everything else.

Share links (nothing is uploaded). A read-only share link carries the data inside the link itself, compressed into the part of the address that is never sent to any server. Creating or opening one involves no upload, no account, and no storage on the reader's device. Anyone who has the link can read what you chose to include, so treat the link like the data it contains — you pick which teams go in and how much history goes with them, and a link only ever carries names, numbers, dates and the fixed reason codes.

Google Sign-In and Sync Were Removed on August 20, 2026

Until that date the app offered an optional "Sign in to sync", which stored your sprint data in a Firebase/Firestore database operated by the developer and recorded your Google account's email address in Firebase Authentication. That feature is gone. The code, the sign-in button and every Google address were removed together, and the page's Content-Security-Policy now names no external origin at all — which is what makes this page's claims checkable rather than a promise. Any account id the old feature left in your browser is deleted the next time you open the app.

If you used sync before that date, a copy of your data may still exist in that database. Email eagleadams86@gmail.com and it, along with the account record holding your email address, will be deleted.

To use the app on more than one device now, use Back up to download a file and restore it on the other device.

What Sprint Velocity Does Not Do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking scripts
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing data with third parties
  • No third-party code of any kind — the chart library is a copy kept in the repository and served from the same address as the page itself
  • No network connections at all: the page's Content-Security-Policy sets connect-src 'none', so the browser itself refuses to let this page open one — not to Google, not to the developer, not anywhere

Your Choices

You can edit or delete anything in the app at any time — Back up → Start again removes everything, and clearing the site's browser data does the same. That is the whole of it: there is no other copy to chase. If you used sync before August 20, 2026, email eagleadams86@gmail.com and the copy left over from then will be deleted.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
